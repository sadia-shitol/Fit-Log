# 🏋️ FitLog

### Track • Plan • Improve

**FitLog** is a responsive workout tracking application built with **Next.js and TypeScript**. It helps users explore exercises, create daily workout plans, save favorite exercises, and track important workout statistics through a clean and responsive interface.

---

## ✨ Overview

FitLog makes it easier to organize and monitor your fitness journey in one place.

Users can browse different exercises, add exercises to their workout plans, save exercises for later, and keep track of important workout information such as **duration, calories burned, and exercise ratings**.

The application is designed with a responsive interface so users can comfortably access their workout information across **desktop, tablet, and mobile devices**.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🏃 **Explore Exercises**
  Browse and discover different exercises and workout activities.

- 📅 **Plan Daily Workouts**
  Add exercises to your daily workout plan and organize your routine.

- 💾 **Save Exercises**
  Save exercises that you want to revisit or include in future workouts.

- ⏱️ **Track Workout Duration**
  Keep track of how long you spend on your workouts.

- 🔥 **Track Calories**
  Monitor estimated calories burned during workouts.

- ⭐ **Exercise Ratings**
  View and track exercise ratings to help understand workout preferences.

- 📊 **Workout Statistics**
  Keep important workout information organized and easy to monitor.

- 📱 **Fully Responsive**
  Designed to provide a smooth experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

- 🎨 **Clean & Modern UI**
  Simple and intuitive interface focused on making workout tracking easy.
